1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
DeepShield evaluates deepfake speech detection robustness under unseen AI
generators and real-world audio degradations (noise, compression, resampling,
and telephone bandpass filtering).

2. EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Audio Standardization : 16,000 Hz Mono, Peak Amplitude Normalized
- Feature Extractor    : 128-bin Log-Mel Spectrogram (dB scaled, Z-score normalized)
- Evaluated Models     : 2D CNN Baseline (356k params) vs Advanced CRNN (2.9M params)
- Protocol             : Generator-Disjoint Splitting (Unseen Generator C in Test Only)
